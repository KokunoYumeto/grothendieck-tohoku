\LayerTarget{tohoku:construction:composite-resolving-functor-spectral-sequence}
Continue to let $\mathbf F$ be a resolving functor for the left exact functor
$F:C\longrightarrow C'$. Let $G$ be a covariant functor from $C'$ to an
abelian category $C''$, and suppose every object of $C'$ is isomorphic to a
subobject of an injective object. For every $A\in C$, consider the second
hyperhomology spectral sequence of $G$ with respect to the complex
$\mathbf F(A)$. It is a spectral functor of $A$, and Proposition 2.5.1 gives
its initial term:
\LayerTarget{tohoku:eq:2.5.6}
\[
\tag{2.5.6}
{}_{\mathrm{II}}E_2^{p,q}G(\mathbf F(A))=R^pG(R^qF(A)).
\]
This is a natural isomorphism with the initial term of the second spectral
sequence of the composite functor $GF$.

\LayerTarget{tohoku:prop:2.5.4}
\noindent\textsc{Proposition 2.5.4.} --- \textit{The isomorphism (2.5.6)
arises from a natural isomorphism of the second hyperhomology spectral
sequence of $G$ with respect to $\mathbf F(A)$ with the second spectral
sequence of the composite functor $GF$.}

\LayerTarget{tohoku:prop:2.5.4:proof}
Let $C(A)$ be an injective resolution of $A$. Regard $F(C(A))$ as a simple
complex in $C'$, and consider the natural homomorphisms (2.5.1). They induce
homomorphisms of the second hyperhomology spectral sequences of $G$:
\[
\mathrm{II}G(F(C(A)))\longrightarrow\mathrm{II}G(\mathbf F(C(A)))
\longleftarrow\mathrm{II}G(\mathbf F(A)).
\]
It remains to show that these are isomorphisms, for which it suffices to show
that the corresponding homomorphisms of initial terms are isomorphisms. By
Proposition 2.5.1,
\[
HF(C(A))\longrightarrow H\mathbf F(C(A))\longleftarrow H\mathbf F(A)
\]
are isomorphisms, which proves the assertion.
