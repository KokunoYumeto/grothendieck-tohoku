\LayerTarget{tohoku:def:complete-subcategory}
Suppose that $\mathbf{C}$ is an abelian category. We call $\mathbf{C}'$ a \textit{complete}
subcategory if (i) \textit{for $A,B\in \mathbf{C}'$ one has
$\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{C}'}(A,B)=\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{C}}(A,B)$}; and (ii) \textit{if, in an
exact sequence $A\to B\to C\to
D\to E$, the four outer terms lie in $\mathbf{C}'$, then the middle term
$C$ also lies in $\mathbf{C}'$.} By (i), $\mathbf{C}'$ is completely determined by its class of
objects. Condition (ii) is equivalent to saying that for every morphism
$P\to Q$ with $P,Q\in \mathbf{C}'$, the kernel and cokernel lie in $\mathbf{C}'$,
and that for every exact sequence
$0\to R'\to R\to R''\to 0$
with $R',R''\in \mathbf{C}'$, one has $R\in \mathbf{C}'$. It follows immediately that $\mathbf{C}'$ is
itself an abelian class and that, for a morphism $u:A\to B$ in
$\mathbf{C}'$, its kernel and cokernel, and hence also its image and coimage, are
identical to the kernel and cokernel (respectively, image and coimage) computed in $\mathbf{C}$.

\LayerTarget{tohoku:def:thick-subcategory}
The subcategory $\mathbf{C}'$ of $\mathbf{C}$ is called \textit{thick} if it satisfies condition
(i) above and the following strengthening of condition (ii): (iii)
\textit{if, in an exact sequence $A\to B\to C$, the
outer terms $A,C$ lie in $\mathbf{C}'$, then $B$ also lies in $\mathbf{C}'$.} If $\mathbf{C}$ is the
abelian category of abelian groups, this gives the notion of a ``class of
abelian groups'' in [17]. As in [17], condition (iii) is equivalent to the
conjunction of the following three conditions: every zero object lies in
$\mathbf{C}'$; every object isomorphic to a subobject or quotient object of an object
of $\mathbf{C}'$ lies in $\mathbf{C}'$; and every extension of two objects of $\mathbf{C}'$ lies in
$\mathbf{C}'$.

\LayerTarget{tohoku:def:quotient-category}
Let $\mathbf{C}$ be an abelian category and $\mathbf{C}'$ a thick subcategory. We shall define a
new abelian category, denoted $\mathbf{C}/\mathbf{C}'$ and called the \textit{quotient category}
of $\mathbf{C}$ by $\mathbf{C}'$. The objects of $\mathbf{C}/\mathbf{C}'$ are, by definition, the objects of
$\mathbf{C}$. We shall define the morphisms from $A$ to $B$ in $\mathbf{C}/\mathbf{C}'$, called
``morphisms modulo $\mathbf{C}'$ from $A$ to $B$''.
\LayerTarget{tohoku:def:quasi-equal}
Say that a subobject $A'$ of $A$ is equal to $A$ modulo $\mathbf{C}'$, or
quasi-equal to $A$, if $A/A'\in \mathbf{C}'$. Every subobject of $A$
containing $A'$ is then again quasi-equal to $A$, and the infimum of two
subobjects of $A$ quasi-equal to $A$ is again quasi-equal to $A$. Dually, we
introduce the notion of a quotient of $B$ quasi-equal to $B$: such a quotient
$B/N$ is quasi-equal to $B$ if $N\in \mathbf{C}'$.
\LayerTarget{tohoku:def:quotient-morphism}
A morphism from $A$ to $B$ modulo $\mathbf{C}'$ is represented by a morphism $f'$ from
a subobject $A'$ of $A$ quasi-equal to $A$ to a quotient $B'$ of $B$
quasi-equal to $B$. A morphism $f'':A''\to B''$ satisfying the
same conditions represents the same morphism modulo $\mathbf{C}'$ if and only if
there exist $A'''\leqq\mathrm{Inf}(A',A'')$ and
$B'''\leqq\mathrm{Inf}(B',B'')$, with $A'''$ quasi-equal to $A$ and $B'''$
quasi-equal to $B$, such that the morphisms $A'''\to B'''$
induced by $f'$ and $f''$ are identical. This relation between $f'$ and $f''$
is indeed an equivalence relation, so the preceding definition of morphisms
modulo $\mathbf{C}'$ is coherent.
\LayerTarget{tohoku:def:quotient-hom}
Suppose that, for every $A\in \mathbf{C}$, the subobjects of $A$ form a set, as is true
for all known categories. We may then consider the set of morphisms from $A$
to $B$ modulo $\mathbf{C}'$, denoted $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{C}/\mathrm{C}'}(A,B)$. It appears as the
inductive limit of the abelian groups $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{C}}(A',B')$, where $A'$ ranges
over the subobjects of $A$ quasi-equal to $A$ and $B'$ ranges over the
quotients of $B$ quasi-equal to $B$; consequently it may be regarded as an
abelian group.
